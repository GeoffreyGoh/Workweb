# Letterhead logos

One per company, named after its code:

    logo_GB.png     PT Graha Blinds Nusantara
    logo_MJ.png     PT Mitra Jendela Indah
    logo_AI.png     CV Anugerah Interior

Roughly 3:1 landscape; scaled to 16 mm tall on the PDF. A company with no file
here prints its name in type instead, so the documents stay usable until the
artwork arrives.

The filename is stored on the company record (`logo_filename`), so it can be
anything - the `logo_<CODE>.png` convention is just the fallback. `logo.png`
is the last resort, shared by any company without its own.
